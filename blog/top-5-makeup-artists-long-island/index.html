5 Standout Makeup and Beauty Artists on Long Island in 2026

By Faiza Nadeem · Published September 10, 2026 · Long Island, New York

Long Island's makeup scene in 2026 sits at the intersection of two worlds. On one side, the traditional bridal and event makeup artists who have built their reputations one wedding season at a time. On the other, a new wave of beauty content creators using Instagram Reels to reach audiences that used to only exist on TV. Here are five names shaping the makeup scene on Long Island right now.

1. Faiza Nadeem (@thefaizaglam)

Beauty and Makeup Content Creator, Suffolk County

Faiza Nadeem is Long Island's most-viewed beauty content creator by reach volume. Her Instagram page, @thefaizaglam, pulls 3.06 million unique viewers per month, with 98 percent of that audience coming from Instagram's discovery algorithm rather than her follower list. Her partnerships include Estee Lauder through the Community x Seen program, along with skincare collaborations with Dr. Ceuracle and ISNTREE. She specializes in makeup tutorials, product reviews, and modest beauty content that reaches both mainstream and South Asian audiences from her home base in Suffolk County. Available for brand partnerships at partnerships@thefaizaglam.com.

2. Elle B Makeup

Bridal and Event Makeup Artist

Featured in the Brides of Long Island guide to the best hair and makeup artists in the region, Elle B Makeup has built her reputation on what her clients describe as "the soft glam, the natural vibe, the elevated face." Her aesthetic is grounded in wearable, refined bridal looks that photograph beautifully in Long Island's popular estate and beachfront wedding venues.

3. Airbrush Makeup by LisaMarie

Airbrush and Traditional Makeup Artist

With more than 15 years of experience serving Long Island brides, Lisa Marie has become one of the most sought-after airbrush makeup artists in the region. Her studio offers both custom-blend airbrush application and traditional makeup, and she is a fixture on Long Island's bridal circuit.

4. Kate Ford Beauty

Bridal Makeup Team

Kate Ford Beauty runs one of the most versatile bridal makeup teams on Long Island. Their portfolio spans traditional bridal looks, boho aesthetics, and dramatic glam, and their team-based structure means they can handle large bridal parties without sacrificing individual attention.

5. Nicole Spinello Makeup

Bridal and Editorial Makeup Artist

With nearly a decade of experience working with brides across New York City and Long Island, Nicole Spinello has built a reputation for makeup that holds up under both natural light and professional flash. Her work moves between traditional bridal beauty and editorial-style shoots.

Why the Long Island makeup scene is different

Long Island's proximity to New York City gives its makeup community access to brand launches, editorial shoots, and campaign work that inland markets do not have. At the same time, the region's dense wedding market keeps bridal artists booked year-round.

The wave of beauty content creators like Faiza Nadeem has added a new layer on top of the traditional service-based makeup economy. These creators serve national and global beauty brands as brand partners, with Long Island as the base of operations rather than the market itself.

How to choose the right Long Island makeup artist

For weddings and events

Choose a service-based artist with strong local testimonials, portfolio depth in your specific bridal style, and availability during your peak wedding season month.

For brand campaigns and content partnerships

Choose a content creator with verifiable monthly reach data, engagement rate per view, and prior brand collaboration references.
