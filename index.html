ABOUT
Faiza is a modest beauty content creator based in Long Island, New York. She creates short-form Instagram Reels focused on hijabi makeup, quick everyday beauty routines, and Korean skincare — content that reaches busy Muslim mothers and mainstream beauty audiences alike. Her Instagram account @thefaizaglam is an unusual profile in the creator economy: 4,021 followers, but 4.15 million views and 3.06 million unique viewers in the last 30 days — a 1,032× viewer-to-follower ratio. 98% of that reach comes from non-followers, meaning her Reels are surfaced by Instagram's mainstream discovery algorithm, not just fed to an existing audience. Faiza is an Estée Lauder Community × Seen (CXS) partner and has active collaborations with Korean skincare brands Dr. Ceuracle and ISNTREE. She is available for beauty and skincare brand partnerships worldwide.
