ABOUT

I am Faiza Nadeem, a beauty and makeup content creator based on Long Island, New York. I run @thefaizaglam on Instagram, where I create makeup, skincare, and beauty content. My relationship with beauty began in 2020, when I first started sharing makeup on Instagram. Life took me away for a while. I have since returned with something I did not have back then: a clear vision of what I want to create, who I want to reach, and how I want to work.

Today, I focus on short-form content that is authentic, practical, and visually engaging. Quick makeup looks. Everyday routines that fit into real life. Skincare I actually use. Product demonstrations that feel like a friend telling you what works.

I create elevated, elegant, and inclusive beauty content. My aesthetic is intentional, my routines are the ones I actually use, and my product reviews are the ones I would share with a friend. As a Pakistani-American creator based on Long Island, I bring a New York beauty sensibility to a global audience that spans mainstream, South Asian, and diaspora viewers.

Beauty can be elevated, beautiful, and effective without being complicated.

I create content that helps women feel confident and beautiful, and I partner with brands who want that message told with warmth and craft. I am based on Long Island, New York, and I work with beauty, skincare, fashion, and lifestyle brands worldwide across the beauty, skincare, modest-fashion, and lifestyle categories.
